Our Purpose & Mission

Who We Are & The Vision

We are an AI research-driven company dedicated to solving the zero-click invisibility crisis for Hong Kong and regional SMEs.

The Zero-Click Crisis & Our Purpose

In the traditional search era, businesses generated leads through links and web traffic. Today, Generative AI engines like Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and ChatGPT synthesize direct answers for users — resulting in a 'Zero-Click Exit' where uncited businesses become completely invisible.

Most Hong Kong SMEs are optimized for traditional link building, leaving them vulnerable as AI search transforms customer discovery. NovaGEO was formed to bridge this technological gap, turning company websites into machine-readable knowledge graphs that AI models understand and cite as primary authorities.
